# Starfall Miner

**Build preference:** Do not build or export an Android APK/AAB unless the user explicitly requests it. Gameplay development and verification should use local C# builds, headless regressions, and rendered desktop runs. An earlier request to produce an APK does not authorize packaging later changes.

Godot 4.7.2 **.NET**, .NET 10 SDK, C#. Desktop targets net10.0; the official prebuilt Android template requires net9.0. Start at `scenes/main.tscn` → `scripts/Main.cs`.

- `Main`: simulation, weapons, economy, loot, progression. `MiningPlanet`: seeded typed grid and damage.
- `AutopilotSteering` / `AutopilotSettings`: mothership decisions use forward first-hit rays and near-field collision clearance only. No planet-centered orbit, global nearest-ore acquisition, hidden geology or minimap-driven targeting. Preserve scan/approach/brake/mine/avoid/recovery behavior, speed bounds, swept collision checks and manual-cut terrain on handoff. A stalled retreat in a tight pocket must widen exposed forward faces instead of waiting forever for the preferred stand-off. Keep its clearance watchdog local and preserve collision/speed bounds. Tune `resources/autopilot_flight.tres`.
- `SurveyMinimap`: read-only cached terrain survey with ship/drone markers. Camera-only redraws must not invalidate terrain topology; no steering may begin through the minimap. Larger planets use radius 54 + 6 × log2(sector) and dynamic generation extents. Preserve exact existing saved worlds and validate extended coordinates. Render visible cells and gather local blast regions rather than scanning the whole planet per wave tick.
- `FlightInput` / `TouchButton`: independent finger ownership. `WorldView`: camera and orientation only.
- `Hud` / `Visuals` / `MiningSound`: presentation. `SaveStore`: explicit legacy v1 JSON schema.
- `Main.Progression` / `EquipmentState`: recipes, material inventory, unique item/mount ownership, market transactions, credit upgrades, ranks and drone mining. `Hud.Workshop` / `ShipSchematic`: paged workshop controls with a persistent fitted-hull preview. Recipes and rank thresholds are authored in `resources/crafting_catalog.tres`; deposit resistance/depths and accelerating sector growth in `resources/planet_generation.tres`.
- Opening progression uses affordable first purchases with substantial Power gains (+60% base damage per level), then steeper prices; Rate gains remain front-loaded. Tune `resources/progression.tres`; research uses the higher of sector and saved lifetime-mined milestones in the catalog. Manual ship output is 1.5× damage / 1.6× cadence (2.4× nominal), with real held-input throughput coverage; never grant drones a mode-switch buff. `tools/dev.py balance --label current --minutes 60` runs an isolated production-simulation pacing profile.
- Red impact charges detonate on positive damage, use an aimed radius preview and a matching bright blast, and drain chains iteratively. Never hide a charge inside a rare resource. Preserve exact rewards and single planet-clear events; keep particle/ring budgets bounded.
- Flux beam (weapon type 5) is continuous, uses elapsed-time damage and exact first-face ray/grid intersections, and in manual mode aims precisely through the held pointer independently of navigation snapping/ship turn. Carry unused laser frame damage only onto newly exposed first-hit faces with bounded traversal. Preserve Power/Rate/overdrive/manual scaling on primary/auxiliary/drone mounts and immediate cancellation on release, modals, resize and focus loss. Keep one sustained laser audio voice across frames and stop it on control cancellation/pause/mute. Existing weapon IDs 0–4 remain stable. Nova keeps circular damage with translucent blue rough-edged presentation and bounded/reused drawing buffers. Sector rock hues vary deterministically; rare/resource color codes remain fixed.
- Use `double` for health/currency/credit totals, `long` for accumulated resource counters, Godot vectors for positions. Avoid per-frame LINQ, dictionary-shaped entities, or fresh collections.
- Mode changes must preserve the live planet, ship, projectiles, and debris. Never reload the scene to rotate.
- Both modes remain portrait. Manual play requires a held world pointer: press starts steering, dragging changes direction, release/cancellation stops. Recompute the steering point as the camera follows, so a stationary held thumb keeps moving beyond its original world position. `ManualNavigation` slows to a safe stand-off while firing at blocking rocks, then continues. Tune speed/clearance in `resources/manual_flight.tres`. Do not replace this with released-tap waypoints, sticks, a separate fire control, or mode-driven window resizing without an explicit design change.
- HUD presentation uses open holographic telemetry and `HologramChannel` modules. `resources/mode_transition.tres` owns the 3-second ignition budget/stagger/overshoot/jitter (abrupt neon strikes, blackouts and stepped displacement; avoid smooth breathing pulses); `resources/hologram_style.tres` owns ink and vector halos. Workshop tabs share one entry ignition per visit. Reselection, tab/category changes, paging, inventory refreshes and resizing must stay settled. Keep activation responsive, bounded, and independent of simulation. Settled modules stop processing; do not reintroduce whole-HUD slide/fade entrances or large per-frame blur effects.
- World designs are `PlanetVisualStyle` resources registered in `Main.VisualStyles` and compared from pause. Keep the reference-inspired Clean Frontier simple; color is independent of geometry. A visual switch must preserve the live planet, tile health, collision, generation settings, wallet, ship and pending rewards. Only Clean Frontier (Default) and Original/Classic Grid are supported; the other presets and rendering branches were scrapped by explicit user request. Removed saved style IDs must fall back to Default without changing simulation data. Resource cells are whole color-coded squares, with no inset badges/ore symbols; only selected energy cells get a shared bounded fusion-core effect. Keep stable style IDs and the optional `visual_style` save field backward compatible. Reuse drawing buffers and cull tiles; do not create per-cell scene nodes.

`GODOT4` overrides the engine executable. VS Code tasks and launch configs are included. See README for template/toolchain setup. Export configurations exclude test code and QA scenes. Do not use browser output as C# validation.

Touch requirements: inject real `InputEventScreenTouch` / `InputEventScreenDrag` through `Viewport.PushInput`. Test movement while the finger remains DOWN, drag-to-turn, continued steering under a stationary thumb as the camera follows, release-to-stop, and mining stand-off/slowdown. Also test the engine mouse-input path at scaled window sizes, not just a direct Viewport click that sets a flag. Menu touches must work while steering, and purchases must charge once despite synthesized mouse events. Canceled/outside releases must stop steering and must not activate buttons. Modals must block controls immediately, including during deferred rebuilds. Do not replace the steering finger with another finger. Clear ownership, navigation, and menu presses on resize, mode change, pause, and focus loss. Weapon selection may retain held steering.

Keep `user://starfall_save_v1.json`, `version: 1`, and serialized names compatible with 0.1/0.2 fixtures. Preserve remaining block health and pending pickup rewards exactly once. Tests/inspection must never touch a player's save. Do not run persistence suites concurrently.

Crafting/trades/upgrades must validate the full transaction before spending. Never mount one weapon twice or leave the ship unarmed. The mothership has one primary plus up to two purchased auxiliary hardpoints; each drone has one mount. Drones cruise independent mining routes and must recall/dock before chassis upgrades, mounted-weapon upgrades or weapon transfers. Recall transfers may continue during paused workshop servicing without mining or collecting rewards. Persist their recall/deploy orders. Drones require fitted weapons and use real weapon behavior; they must not silently mine while unarmed or paused. Weapon fire-rate tuning is a separate saved `RateLevel` track; apply its cadence to primary, auxiliary and drone mounts, preserve defaults for earlier saves and retain docking/rank/atomic-spend rules. Save rare-fragment payloads with inventory so collection cannot duplicate rewards. Preserve legacy weapon access, shared upgrade levels (now diminishing bonus formulas), and exact saved worlds; new generation uses the new economy. Offline income awards credits only, and market sales must not inflate its observed mining rate.

Keep Android ID `com.starfall.miner.demo`; increase version code for a new release. Never claim update compatibility without matching certificates. Never commit keystores, export credentials, generated caches, or SDKs. Describe injected touch, desktop resizing, and physical Android testing separately. Record actual rendering inspection and platform limitations in QA_REPORT.md.
